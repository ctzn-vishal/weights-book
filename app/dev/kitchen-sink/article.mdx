## Math

The Kish effective sample size is $$\nKish = n/(1+\CV(w)^2)$$, and the design effect compares two variances of the same estimator:

$$
\hat\theta = \frac{\sum_{i \in s} w_i\, y_i}{\sum_{i \in s} w_i}, \qquad \DEFF(\hat\theta) = \frac{\Var_{\text{design}}(\hat\theta)}{\Var_{\text{SRS}}(\hat\theta)}
$$

In the fixture, the design effect is <Fact id="dev.deff" /> and the Kish size is <Fact id="dev.n_kish" />.
